# 💡 Lumi – Articulated Robotic Lamp

## 📌 Overview

**Lumi** is an articulated robotic lamp prototype designed and developed during my internship at **Finstein Robotics**. The project focused primarily on the **mechanical design, 3D modelling, fabrication, assembly, and basic motor actuation** of the lamp.

The complete mechanical structure was designed from scratch using **Fusion 360**, incorporating an articulated multi-segment body and a movable lamp head mounted on a rotating base. The individual components were prepared for 3D printing and subsequently assembled with servo motors to demonstrate the intended movement of the mechanism.

The project was developed as an initial prototype. Further development of electronics, control algorithms, sensing, and advanced functionality was left to the company. My contribution focused on establishing the **mechanical design and initial actuation concept**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- To develop an articulated robotic lamp concept from scratch.
- To design the mechanical components using Fusion 360.
- To create a modular structure suitable for 3D printing.
- To integrate servo motors into the articulated joints.
- To assemble the printed components into a functional mechanical prototype.
- To demonstrate the basic movement and actuation of the designed mechanism.
- To establish an initial mechanical foundation for further development by the company.

## 🧰 Design & Development

### 1. Concept Development

The initial concept was developed as an **articulated robotic lamp**, where the lamp head could be positioned through multiple movable joints.

The design was intended to provide flexibility in positioning the light source while maintaining a compact desktop form factor.

```text
             ┌─────────────┐
             │  Lamp Head  │
             └──────┬──────┘
                    │
                 Joint

              └─────┬─────┘
                    │
                 Joint
                    │

              │  Segment  │
              └─────┬─────┘
                    │
                 Joint
                    │
              ┌─────┴─────┐
              │   Base    │
              └───────────┘
```

## 🖥️ CAD Design

The complete mechanical structure was modelled using **Fusion 360**.

The design consisted of individually modelled components that were combined into an assembly to verify:

- Joint arrangement
- Component alignment
- Servo placement
- Range of movement
- Assembly compatibility
- Overall structural form

The modular approach also allowed individual components to be fabricated, assembled, and replaced independently when required.

---

## 🖨️ 3D Printing

After completing the CAD design, the individual mechanical components were prepared for **3D printing**.

The fabrication process involved:

1. Finalizing the CAD components.
2. Preparing the parts for fabrication.
3. 3D printing the individual mechanical components.
4. Inspecting the printed parts.
5. Removing print supports where required.
6. Checking dimensional compatibility.
7. Preparing the components for assembly.

---

## 🔧 Mechanical Assembly

The printed components were assembled to create the complete articulated structure.

The assembly included:

- Base mechanism
- Articulated arm segments
- Joint components
- Servo motor mounts
- Lamp/head assembly

Special attention was given to joint alignment and motor mounting to ensure that the articulated sections could move without excessive mechanical interference.

---

## ⚡ Motor Actuation

Servo motors were integrated into the designed joints to demonstrate the movement capability of the mechanism.

The motors provide rotational actuation at selected joints, allowing the articulated structure to change its configuration.

```text
                Lamp Head
                    │
              Servo Joint
                    │
                    ▼
              Arm Segment
                    │
              Servo Joint
                    │
                    ▼
              Arm Segment
                    │
              Servo Joint
                    │
                    ▼
             Rotating Base
```

The completed assembly was tested to verify basic motor-driven movement of the articulated mechanism.
